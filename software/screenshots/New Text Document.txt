# SmartKissan Software Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots of the SmartKissan web application.

## Dashboard
Displays weather information, farm health, smart alerts, recent activities, and quick access to project modules.

## Disease Detection
Allows farmers to upload a crop leaf image for AI-based disease detection.

## Disease Result
Shows the predicted disease, confidence score, recommended pesticide, dosage, and treatment guidance.

## Analytics Dashboard
Provides insights into disease statistics, scan history, farm health, and overall analytics.

## Weather Dashboard
Displays live weather conditions and recommendations based on environmental data.

## Live Weather Widget
A compact weather card showing temperature, humidity, and weather conditions.

## Dukan Marketplace
Helps farmers locate nearby seed stores, fertilizer shops, pesticide shops, and nurseries.

## Scan Reports
Maintains the history of crop disease scans and generated reports.

## Notification Preferences
Allows users to customize weather alerts and farming notifications.

## Help Center
Provides project guidance and support information.

## Kissan Sahayak AI
An AI-powered chatbot that answers farming-related questions and provides agricultural guidance.
